Features

The codebase is complete as of this writing — no stub functions, no TODO markers found in rogue/*.py. Every system below is implemented and playable.

#

Procedural dungeons

3×3 grid of cells, one randomized room per cell, connected by a randomized spanning tree over the grid graph (plus occasional extra edges for loops). Corridors are L-shaped paths carved through doors.

░

Room-based visibility

Standing in a room lights the whole room; standing in a corridor lights only your immediate tiles — matching the original's lighting rather than modern raycast FOV. Seen-but-dark tiles stay drawn dim.

⟳

Turn-based loop

Every turn-consuming action triggers hunger decay, status-effect countdowns, natural regen, monster AI, and dog AI, in that fixed order, then checks for player death.

⚔

Combat

A simplified percentage to-hit roll plus dice-based damage, standing in for the original's exact hit tables.

)

Weapons & armor

Six weapons, eight armors, each with dice damage or AC values and an optional (initially hidden) +/− enchantment.

!

Potions & scrolls

Nine potion effects, eight scroll effects. Colors and titles are shuffled fresh each game — "blue potion" means something different every run until identified.

%

Hunger clock

Starts at 2000, warns at 300 ("hungry") and 150 ("weak"), then ticks starvation damage every 3 turns once it hits zero.

d

Companion dog

Spawns beside you at game start, follows through stairs, attacks adjacent monsters on its own, and grows from d to a permanently stronger D once fed. Unkillable by design.

*

26-letter bestiary

One species per letter A–Z, danger scaling roughly with letter and spawn weighting toward the current depth.

†

Permadeath, one save slot

The whole game state is pickled to save.dat. That file is deleted on both death and victory — there is no reloading past either.

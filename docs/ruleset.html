Combat

# hit chance, clamped 5–95% hit% = 45 + atk_bonus × 5 − defender_AC × 4 # damage on a hit, minimum 1 dmg = max(1, roll(weapon_dice) + (STR − 16) ÷ 3) # player attack bonus atk_bonus = character_level + weapon_plus

Armor AC is base_ac − armor_plus — lower is better, matching the original's inverted AC direction. Bare-handed damage is 1d3.

Win & lose

Win

Carry the Amulet of Yendor from wherever it's found back up to dungeon level 1.

Lose

HP reaches 0, from combat or starvation. Permadeath — the save file is deleted immediately.
